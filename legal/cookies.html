Cookies Policy

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. In the context of TUNCOIN, we use them to provide a seamless decentralized experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use **Essential Technical Cookies** for the following purposes:

  • Wallet Connection: To remember your connected wallet address so you don't have to re-authenticate every time you navigate between pages.
  • Security: To protect our platform and your session from basic web vulnerabilities.
  • User Preferences: To save your settings, such as "Dark Mode" or language preferences.

3. No Tracking or Advertising

Unlike traditional websites, TUNCOIN does not use "Tracking Cookies" or "Advertising Cookies". We do not track your behavior across other websites, and we do not sell your data to third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Analytics

We may use basic decentralized analytics tools (like Plausible or similar privacy-focused tools) to monitor website performance and traffic without identifying individual users.

5. Managing Your Cookies

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings. However, please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent you from connecting your wallet or interacting with our Smart Contracts correctly.

6. Updates to this Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect technical changes in our platform. We recommend checking this page periodically.
