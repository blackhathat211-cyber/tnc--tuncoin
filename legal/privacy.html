Privacy Policy

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional platforms, TUNCOIN does not collect personal identification information (PII). We do not ask for, or store, your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

2. Wallet Connectivity

Our platform interacts with your public wallet address (e.g., MetaMask) to display your TNC balance and transaction history. This process is decentralized and occurs locally on your browser.

3. Private Keys & Security

CRITICAL: We never have access to your private keys, seed phrases, or passwords. You are the sole custodian of your digital assets. We will never ask you to provide this information under any circumstances.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential technical cookies to remember your wallet connection status. This is only to enhance your experience so you don't have to reconnect every time you refresh the page.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to decentralized exchanges (like PancakeSwap) or block explorers (like BscScan). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms.

6. Updates

We may update this policy as the TUNCOIN ecosystem evolves. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the current policy.
